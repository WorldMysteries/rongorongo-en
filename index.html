«Talking Boards» of Easter Island: Carving, Reverse Boustrophedon and Epigraphy

Kohau Rongorongo is the unique indigenous script of Polynesia, carved with obsidian flakes, the mata'a, and with a shark tooth into fluted tablets of toromiro wood. The tablets are read by the method of reverse boustrophedon: on reaching the right-hand end of a line, the tangata rongorongo chanter turned the tablet upside down by 180 degrees.

The Science of Rongorongo · From St Petersburg to Rapa Nui

Keys to Decipherment and Live Tablet Statistics

In 1871 Nikolai Nikolaevich Miklouho-Maclay acquired two Rongorongo tablets, now kept in the Kunstkamera (MAE RAS, St Petersburg). In 1938 the Leningrad schoolboy Boris Kudryavtsev discovered that one and the same sacred text, with telling replacements of single signs by ligatures, is written on the St Petersburg tablets P and Q and on the Great Santiago Tablet H. The only passage of Rongorongo whose functional meaning is accepted by all researchers (T. Barthel, J. Guy) is the astronomical calendar of the nights of the Moon on tablet C «Mamari» (rows Ca6–Ca9). It lists the 28–30 nights of the synodic month from the new moon Ohiro to the full moon Omotohi, indicating the favorable nights for deep-sea fishing. Choose a phase:

The Santiago Staff (tablet I) contains 2,320 glyphs divided by the vertical staff B-901. Yu. V. Knorozov, N. A. Butinov and S. Fischer showed that many segments form genealogical or cosmogonic creation triads following the formula of the Rapanui chants «He timo te ako-ako»: «The progenitor X united with Y — Z was born».
